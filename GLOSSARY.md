# FirstDay

FirstDay helps new joiners find onboarding knowledge for five job roles.

## Language

**Learner**:
A person using FirstDay to read onboarding knowledge and ask questions as they join an organization or take on a role.
_Avoid_: Student, trainee

**Administrator**:
A person responsible for adding and maintaining the onboarding knowledge available to learners.
_Avoid_: Superuser, content uploader

**Knowledge document**:
A maintained source of onboarding information used to support learner answers.
_Avoid_: Memory, AI knowledge

**Job role**:
One of FirstDay's five onboarding audiences: Frontend Developer, Backend Developer, Designer, Business Analyst, or HR.
_Avoid_: Department, access role

**Onboarding checklist**:
An administrator-defined set of tasks for a job role that learners mark complete themselves.
_Avoid_: Approval workflow

**Document version**:
A particular revision of a knowledge document, kept distinct from other revisions of the same document.
_Avoid_: Duplicate document

**Active version**:
The fully indexed document version currently available to learners and used to support answers.
_Avoid_: Latest upload, pending version

**Checklist task**:
An onboarding action within a job role's checklist that a learner can mark complete or incomplete.
_Avoid_: Approval request

**Onboarding stage**:
A suggested grouping of checklist tasks: Before you start, Day 1, or Week 1. A stage guides sequence rather than setting a deadline.
_Avoid_: Due date

**Checklist progress**:
The number and proportion of a job role's checklist tasks that a learner has completed, with every task counted equally.
_Avoid_: Performance score

**Question draft**:
A learner’s unfinished, unsubmitted question associated with a particular chat search scope.
_Avoid_: Conversation history

**Checklist revision**:
A saved edition of a job role’s onboarding checklist, defining the tasks and order currently presented to learners.
_Avoid_: Document version

**Suggested question**:
An administrator-written question offered with a checklist task for the learner to review, edit and submit.
_Avoid_: Automatic answer

**Chat search scope**:
The job role or All roles selection that determines which published knowledge is relevant to a chat request.
_Avoid_: Access permission
